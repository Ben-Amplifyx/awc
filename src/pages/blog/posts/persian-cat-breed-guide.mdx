## Overview

Persian cats are one of the most popular and recognizable cat breeds in the world. Known for their luxurious long coats and sweet, gentle personalities, Persians make wonderful companions.

## Physical Characteristics

### Appearance
- **Size**: Medium to large
- **Weight**: 7-12 pounds
- **Coat**: Long, thick, and flowing
- **Colors**: Available in many colors and patterns
- **Eyes**: Large, round, and expressive

### Distinctive Features
- Flat face (brachycephalic)
- Short muzzle
- Small ears
- Stocky body

## Personality & Temperament

Persian cats are known for being:
- Calm and gentle
- Affectionate with family
- Quiet and soft-voiced
- Prefer peaceful environments
- Great lap cats

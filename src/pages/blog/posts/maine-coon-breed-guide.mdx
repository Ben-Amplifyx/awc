## Overview

The Maine Coon is one of the largest domestic cat breeds and is known for its friendly, sociable nature. These "gentle giants" are beloved for their dog-like personalities and impressive size.

## Physical Characteristics

### Appearance
- **Size**: Large to extra-large
- **Weight**: Males 13-18 pounds, Females 8-12 pounds
- **Coat**: Long, shaggy, water-resistant
- **Colors**: Many colors and patterns
- **Eyes**: Large, expressive

### Distinctive Features
- Large, tufted ears
- Bushy tail
- Large paws with tufts
- Rectangular body shape
- Ruff around neck

## Personality & Temperament

Maine Coons are known for being:
- Friendly and social
- Playful throughout life
- Good with children and other pets
- Intelligent and trainable
- "Dog-like" in behavior

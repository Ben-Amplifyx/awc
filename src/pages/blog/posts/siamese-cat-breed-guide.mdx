## Overview

Siamese cats are one of the most recognizable and ancient cat breeds. Originating from Thailand (formerly Siam), these elegant cats are known for their distinctive appearance and talkative personalities.

## Physical Characteristics

### Appearance
- **Size**: Medium
- **Weight**: 8-12 pounds
- **Coat**: Short, fine, glossy
- **Colors**: Color-point pattern (seal, chocolate, blue, lilac)
- **Eyes**: Striking blue, almond-shaped

### Distinctive Features
- Wedge-shaped head
- Large ears
- Slender, athletic build
- Long, tapering tail
- Color-point pattern (darker face, ears, paws, tail)

## Personality & Temperament

Siamese cats are known for being:
- Extremely vocal and talkative
- Highly social and affectionate
- Intelligent and curious
- Demanding of attention
- Strong bonds with humans
